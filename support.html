Support

Email info@strangeglyph.com and a person will answer.

Dive Tend is made by one person. There is no ticket queue and no bot — mail goes to a real inbox and gets a real reply, usually within a couple of days.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything except cloud backup works with no account at all, and your locker lives on your phone. An account is created only if you start a trial or subscribe.

Where is my data?

On the free plan, on your device and nowhere else. That is deliberate — but it means if you lose the phone without exporting, the data is gone. Export is free on every plan and includes your photos: Settings → Export my data.

What does Premium add?

Cloud backup and sync across your devices, unlimited gear and photos, push notifications, and no ads. The free plan keeps 20 gear items and 3 photos across the whole locker, and shows everything that is due whenever you open the app.

What happens if my subscription lapses?

Nothing is taken away from your phone — ever. Items over the free limits stay visible and usable; you simply cannot add new ones until you are back under the limit or resubscribe. Your cloud copy is kept for 90 days and then deleted. We tell you before that happens so you can export or resubscribe.

Can I get my data out?

Yes, at any time, on any plan. Settings → Export my data produces a readable file of everything plus your photos.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete my account. It is immediate and permanent, and it removes your data from our servers.

The app says something is due — is my gear unsafe?

Dive Tend only knows what you have told it. It tracks the interval you entered, or one the manufacturer publishes that we have recorded with its source and date. It does not inspect your equipment and cannot tell you whether it is safe to dive. Confirm anything that matters with your manufacturer or a certified technician.

Why does the serial scanner miss things?

It reads printed and stamped characters on your device, with no network and no AI guessing. That means it will not invent a reading — but it also means a worn stamp, an awkward angle or poor light can leave it with nothing. Hold the text inside the bright frame, and you can always type the serial in instead.

A recall notice appeared on my gear

We match safety notices on brand and model only, and we cannot tell whether your specific unit is affected. Check your serial number against the official notice and speak to the manufacturer before diving the item.

Something is wrong, or missing

Tell us — info@strangeglyph.com. Bug reports and "I wish it did X" are equally welcome. If you are reporting a bug, your device model and iOS version help.
