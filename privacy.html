Privacy Policy

Strange Glyph, LLC  ·  Last updated 31 October 2026

The short version. Dive Tend works without an account, and on the free plan nothing about your gear leaves your phone — no locker, no photos, no service history. If you subscribe, your locker syncs to your own account so it survives a lost phone. Free users see ads from Google AdMob. We never sell your data, and no advertiser ever sees what is in your locker.

Who we are

Dive Tend is made by Strange Glyph, LLC, a limited liability company registered in Georgia, USA. Strange Glyph, LLC is the data controller. Questions about this policy, or any request under it: info@strangeglyph.com.

The part that matters most: the free plan is local

Dive Tend is built local-first. On the free plan your gear, kits, lists, service history, dive records and photos are stored on your device only. There is no account, and that data is never transmitted to us. If you delete the app without exporting, it is gone — that is the trade local-first makes, and it is why Export is free and always available.

What we collect, and when

If you never create an account

Nothing about your locker. The app still shows ads (see below), and those involve your device and advertising identifiers, which go to Google rather than to us.

If you start a trial or subscribe

An account is created so your locker can be backed up. We then store:

  • Your email address — used to sign you in with a six-digit code, and to send service email such as a notice before cloud backup ends.
  • Your locker — gear, brands, models, serial numbers, purchase details, service history, warranties, kits, lists, dive records and photos. This is yours; we store it so you can get it back.
  • Subscription status — whether you are on a trial, subscribed, or lapsed. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card.

Photos

Photos you attach are resized on your device before anything is uploaded, and only that smaller copy is ever sent. The full-size original stays on your phone and is never replaced. On the free plan no photo is uploaded at all.

Diagnostics

We use PostHog to see which parts of the app are used and to be told when something breaks. PostHog receives app events — a screen opened, an item added, a service logged — and never your gear, serial numbers, photos or notes.

Advertising

Free users see ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect device and advertising identifiers, general device information and approximate location to serve and measure ads. In the EEA and UK you are asked for consent first, and you can decline; in the US you can turn off personalised ads and exercise Do Not Sell or Share, both in Settings → Ads & privacy. Premium subscribers see no ads at all.

No advertiser receives anything you enter into Dive Tend. Your locker is never shared with an ad network — not now, not ever. The ad SDK and your gear data are separate systems that do not meet.

Google is our ad network only. Our analytics are handled by a separate company, PostHog, which receives no advertising identifiers and never sees what is in your locker.

Who else processes your data

  • Supabase — database and authentication for subscriber accounts. Data is stored in the European Union (Ireland). Privacy policy.
  • Apple — processes all purchases. We receive confirmation only.
  • Resend — delivers sign-in codes and service email. Privacy policy.
  • Google (AdMob) — advertising on the free plan. Privacy policy.
  • PostHog — product analytics and error reporting. Privacy policy.

International transfers

Strange Glyph, LLC is a US business, and the database holding subscriber accounts is hosted in the European Union (Ireland). Where any of our processors transfers data outside the EEA or UK, that transfer is made under the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

Your locker is kept while your account exists. If your subscription lapses, the cloud copy is retained for 60 days and then permanently deleted — your device copy is never touched, and we tell you before the window closes so you can export or resubscribe. Deleting your account removes your data from our systems immediately. Your photos and documents are stored separately from the database and are deleted outright — they are not held in any database backup. Database records can persist in our provider's encrypted daily backups for up to 7 days after deletion, after which they age out and are gone.

Your rights

Most of these are buttons in the app rather than requests to us, which is deliberate:

  • Get a copy — Settings → Export my data. Free on every plan, and it includes your photos.
  • Delete everything — Settings → Delete my account. This is immediate and permanent.
  • Correct it — edit any item in the app.
  • Withdraw ad consent — Settings → Ads & privacy.

If you are in the EEA or UK you also have the right to object, to restrict processing, to portability, and to complain to your local supervisory authority. Anything you cannot do in the app, email info@strangeglyph.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

Dive Tend is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. In the EEA and UK the threshold is 16. The free plan transmits nothing to us in any case.

Changes

If we change this materially we will update the date above and, where the change affects what we do with data you have already given us, tell you in the app.
